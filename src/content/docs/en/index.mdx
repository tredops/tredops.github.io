Welcome to the **official TredOps documentation**: the platform that lets you
create, optimise and monitor trading decisions with bots and AI agents, without
giving up control of your funds.

Your capital always stays in your external broker account. TredOps connects
through integrations (Alpaca and others) so the signals your bots and agents
produce can trade with professional risk management.

## Start here

<Steps>

1. **Learn the vocabulary** — [Key concepts](concepts/): portfolio, bot, signal,
   agent, order and calibration in five minutes. And
   [the TredOps loop](lifecycle/): the six checks a strategy clears before it
   touches your money.

2. **Set up your account** — [Quick start](getting-started/): sign-up, broker and
   risk rules.

3. **Pick a strategy with data** — [Benchmark](benchmark/): which agents and
   which models have worked, over the same window and the same capital.

4. **Put something to work** — [Your first bot](guides/first-bot/) and
   [Working with agents](guides/first-agent/).

</Steps>
