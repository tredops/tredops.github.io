The **Continuous Bot Integration** pillar is the creation, optimization, and continuous management layer for trading bots.

TredOps lets you create bots from any available asset using proprietary algorithms. From the platform you can generate a complete bot with these capabilities:

- **Market data** — Real-time and historical OHLCV data for the asset.
- **Optimization** — Parameter tuning via genetic algorithms and proprietary optimization (continuous experimentation and improvement).
- **Performance testing** — Tests over historical periods and evaluation under unexpected market conditions.
- **Quality management** — Only bots that pass minimum quality thresholds go to production; the rest stay in **staging**, running in the background.
- **Automatic calibration** — Each bot has its own calibration schedule and periodically adapts to recent market conditions.
- **Signal Pool** — Every trade generated by a bot goes into a pool of signals that other agents analyze to decide whether it is a real opportunity.

## Calibration

Calibration determines the optimal parameters for an asset and strategy. It is the **source of truth** for evaluating orders.

- Requests are queued automatically for async processing.
- Calibration runs against historical data and stores scores and experiment results.
- When opening a new order, the system looks up the best valid calibration, avoiding *look-ahead bias* (no future data in the evaluation).

## Create bots by sector

You can create bots **in bulk by economic sector**:

1. Pick the economic sector.
2. Filter by exchange and asset type (ETFs, stocks, crypto).
3. Exclude symbols you already cover with your own or public bots.
4. Confirm the batch: TredOps generates, calibrates, and evaluates each bot independently.

Only bots for assets with `active` status are created.

## Search & management

The bots dashboard lets you view, filter, create, and delete bots and their configurations:

- Status tabs: All, Completed, Queued, Calibration, Failed, Public, Private.
- Filters by calibration status, stage (production/staging), and visibility.
- Search by symbol or name.
- **Stop Loss** and **Take Profit** configuration per bot.

## Signal Pool

The **Signal Pool** is the set of trading signals (orders issued by bots) available for analysis. It is the shared layer between bots and agents: each signal can be consumed by agents to decide whether it is a real opportunity before acting.

## The services behind it

This pillar relies on the evaluation engine, the trading engine and the dashboard. You never need to deal with any of them separately — the platform coordinates everything for you.
