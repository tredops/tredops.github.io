## Reading the table

- **Four families, not one market.** Crypto bot platforms (3Commas, Cryptohopper, Pionex) sell pre-built strategies you configure. No-code stock tools (Composer, TradersPost, Trade Ideas) turn rules or alerts into orders. Open-source frameworks (Freqtrade, plus Hummingbot and QuantConnect Lean) give unlimited depth to whoever can code and host them. LLM products and arenas (Alphio, TradeRank, Alpha Arena, TredOps) let language models reason about markets.
- **"AI" means different things.** Trade Ideas' Holly and Cryptohopper's "AI strategies" are rule and statistics engines. Freqtrade's FreqAI is classic machine learning. Alphio, TredOps and the arenas run large language models that read context and justify each decision.
- **Custody is the first filter.** Pionex is the only custodial option: its bots are the cheapest to run precisely because your funds sit on its exchange. Everyone else connects to a broker or exchange account you keep.
- **Coverage vs. depth.** TradersPost and 3Commas win on the number of venues; Composer and Freqtrade on strategy tooling; the arenas on transparency. TredOps combines bot generation, optimisation, quality gating, recalibration and LLM supervision in one loop, on a single broker today.
- **Where the frontier is moving.** Kraken bought Capitalise.ai (August 2025) to put natural-language automation inside an exchange; Alphio launched agentic accounts with Robinhood (July 2026); nof1 raised $15M after Alpha Arena to build a consumer product. Expect every family to add an LLM layer within a year.
