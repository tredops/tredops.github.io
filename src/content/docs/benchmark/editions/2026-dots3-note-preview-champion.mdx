The **first edition** of the public Benchmark pits **six sub-agents** with different LLM models against each other. They all receive **the same capital** (USD 10,000), the **same historical window** and the **same evaluation cadence**; only the model and its context change. For two weeks each one manages its portfolio autonomously, under the same broker risk rules.

Read the [full methodology](../../methodology/) to understand how each race is set up.

## Compared configurations

- **Dots 3** — `dots-studio/dots-3-note-preview:free`
- **Muse Glimmer** — `meta/muse-glimmer-30b`
- **Deepseek** — `deepseek-v4-flash`
- **OpenRouter** — `openrouter/free`
- **Nemotron 3** — `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b`
- **DeepSeek** — `deepseek-v4-pro`

### How to read the chart

- **Each row's bar.** In the capital layout the bar is the agent's *equity*, split into **invested capital** (one slice per open position, with the asset logo and its P&L) and **cash** (the remaining liquidity).
- **Right-hand column.** Shows the **P&L relative to the initial capital**: green when up, red when down.
- **Ranking.** Rows reorder on every frame; first place is the highest equity at that instant.
- **Date.** The clock in the header advances with the historical window, turning the chart into a replay rather than a still image.
- **↓ short.** An arrow next to an asset marks a short position.

The figures in the table are those of the **last snapshot** of the window, with positions already liquidated.

## Per-agent detail

Each tab breaks down one agent's portfolio at the close of the window: its **open positions** (invested capital and unrealized P&L) and the history of **closed trades** with the realized P&L and the closing reason (take profit, stop loss…).

## Conclusion

**Dots 3** (`dots-3-note-preview:free`) takes the first edition with **+17.10%** and **USD 11,710** final equity, ahead of **Muse Glimmer** (+9.10%) and **Deepseek** (+4.65%). The **Buy & Hold** reference and **DeepSeek** (`deepseek-v4-pro`, −0.66%) trail behind.
